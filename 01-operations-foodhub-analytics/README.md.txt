FoodHub Delivery Logistics Analysis Overview: 

Comprehensive diagnostic analysis of 1,898 food delivery orders in New York to optimize customer retention, restaurant partnerships, and courier dispatch logistics.  

Key Findings:65.4% of users churn after a single order; over 80% place only 1–2 orders.  

Order fulfillment is constrained by a predictable ~27.4-minute kitchen prep time, combining with transit to average ~51.5 minutes total fulfillment time.  38.8% of orders lack customer ratings, obscuring quality feedback.
 
Recommendations: Implement dynamic driver dispatch based on kitchen prep milestones, introduce loyalty incentives on 2nd/3rd orders, and optimize app placement for long-tail restaurants.  

Stack: Python, Pandas, NumPy, Seaborn, Matplotlib.
