# ExtraaLearn EdTech Lead Scoring

## Overview
Supervised classification pipeline evaluating 4,612 prospective student leads to predict conversion likelihood and prioritize sales outreach.

## Key Findings
* **Baseline Conversion:** Average conversion rate across all marketing channels is ~29.9%.
* **Occupation Impact:** Working professionals convert at 35.5%, compared to only 11.7% for current students.
* **Channel Performance:** Referral leads deliver a 67.7% conversion rate despite lower volume, while educational channels capture high volume at lower conversion efficiency.

## Models Evaluated
* Logistic Regression
* Decision Trees
* Bagging Classifiers
* Random Forests
* Support Vector Machines (SVM)
* Grid-Search Hyperparameter Optimization

## Tech Stack
* **Language & Libraries:** Python, Scikit-Learn, Pandas, NumPy
