## 🚀 Projects Overview

### 1. [FoodHub Delivery Operations & Logistics Analysis](./01-operations-foodhub-analytics/)
* **Focus:** Exploratory Data Analysis, Workflow Optimization, Customer Retention
* **Tech Stack:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
* **Overview:** Diagnostic analysis of 1,898 food delivery orders in New York to uncover operational bottlenecks and enhance customer lifetime value.
* **Key Results:**
  * Identified a significant retention drop-off, with 65.4% of users ordering only once.
  * Measured an average kitchen preparation time of ~27.4 minutes, contributing to an overall order-to-door cycle of ~51.5 minutes.
  * Highlighted demand concentration among a small tier of top restaurants and proposed targeted driver dispatch and re-engagement strategies.

### 2. [ExtraaLearn EdTech Lead Scoring & Conversion](./02-edtech-lead-scoring-classification/)
* **Focus:** Supervised Classification, Model Evaluation, Hyperparameter Tuning
* **Tech Stack:** Python, Scikit-Learn, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
* **Overview:** Predictive classification pipeline built on 4,612 leads to identify prospective students most likely to convert to paid enrollments and optimize sales resource allocation.
* **Key Results:**
  * Benchmarked multiple models including Decision Trees, Random Forests, Bagging Classifiers, Logistic Regression, and Support Vector Machines (SVM).
  * Found that working professionals convert at 35.5% (compared to 11.7% for students).
  * Showed that referral leads yield a 67.7% conversion rate, identifying key channels for high-ROI marketing spend.

---

### 3. [Amazon Product Recommendation Engine](./03-ecommerce-recommendation-engine/)
* **Focus:** Collaborative Filtering, Matrix Factorization, Personalization
* **Tech Stack:** Python, Scikit-Surprise, Scikit-Learn, Pandas, NumPy
* **Overview:** Multi-model recommendation engine designed to tackle user-item catalog sparsity and choice overload using Amazon product ratings.
* **Key Results:**
  * Built and evaluated Rank-based, User-User Collaborative Filtering, Item-Item Collaborative Filtering, and SVD Matrix Factorization models.
  * Optimized User-User Collaborative Filtering to achieve the best overall performance with an F1@10 score of 87.1% and Recall@10 of 89.9%.
  * Tuned SVD Matrix Factorization to achieve the lowest prediction error with an RMSE of 0.8977.
