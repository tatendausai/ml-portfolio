ExtraaLearn EdTech Lead Scoring Overview: 

Supervised classification pipeline evaluating 4,612 prospective student leads to predict conversion likelihood and prioritize sales outreach.  

Key Findings:Baseline conversion rate across all channels is ~29.9%.  Working professionals convert at 35.5%, compared to only 11.7% for current students.  

Referral leads deliver a 67.7% conversion rate despite lower volume, while educational channels capture high volume at lower conversion efficiency.  

Models Evaluated: Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Bagging, Random Forests, Support Vector Machines (SVM), and Grid-Search Hyperparameter Optimization.  

Stack: Python, Scikit-Learn, Pandas, NumPy.
