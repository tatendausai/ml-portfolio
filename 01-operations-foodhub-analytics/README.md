# FoodHub Delivery Logistics Analysis

## Overview
Comprehensive diagnostic analysis of 1,898 food delivery orders in New York to optimize customer retention, restaurant partnerships, and courier dispatch logistics.

## Key Findings
* **Customer Retention:** 65.4% of users churn after a single order; over 80% place only 1–2 orders.
* **Order Fulfillment:** Constrained by a predictable ~27.4-minute kitchen prep time, combining with transit to average ~51.5 minutes total fulfillment time.
* **Customer Feedback:** 38.8% of orders lack customer ratings, obscuring quality feedback.

## Recommendations
* Implement dynamic driver dispatch based on kitchen prep milestones.
* Introduce loyalty incentives on 2nd/3rd orders.
* Optimize app placement for long-tail restaurants.

## Tech Stack
* **Language & Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Seaborn, Matplotlib
